# Privacy Policy and Cookies – vAlpha 

This Privacy Policy outlines how user data is collected, processed, and protected when using vAlpha (valpha.pl).

## 1. Data Controller
The data controller for personal data collected through the Platform is Juliusz Samoraj, e-mail: support@valpha.pl.

## 2. Purposes of Data Processing
We process your data for the following reasons:
1. **Account Creation and Maintenance:** We process your email and password to secure your login and allow access to your profile.
2. **Analytics and App Optimization:** We process anonymized user behavior patterns to improve vAlpha's functionality.

## 3. Third-Party Processors
Your data is strictly protected, but to ensure high performance, vAlpha cooperates with the following trusted services:
1. **Supabase** – cloud database and user authentication system (where your profile credentials reside securely).
2. **Stripe** – payment infrastructure. vAlpha Manager NEVER stores or sees your credit card credentials. All checkouts happen directly on Stripe's secure environment.
3. **Google Analytics 4** – Used to track general traffic statistics. This data is entirely anonymized.

## 4. User Rights
Under GDPR and privacy regulations, you have the right to:
1. Access, correct, or update your personal information.
2. Request full deletion of your account and all associated metrics ("Right to be forgotten").
3. Object to anonymous analytic data processing.
To exercise any of these options, contact us at support@valpha.pl.

## 5. Cookies
The Platform utilizes cookies to maintain active user sessions and track anonymous interaction behaviors via Google Analytics 4. You can block or delete cookies in your web browser settings.
